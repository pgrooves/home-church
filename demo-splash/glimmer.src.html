Bright pass

A catch of light on metal

The same sweep narrowed, brightened, and cut to 780ms, with a small gold bloom under the house at the peak. It announces itself, which is either the point or the problem. Worth watching in dark, where it has the most room to shine, and then in light, where it has the least.

The beats, as they run now

The sweep sits where it does on purpose: late enough that the house has finished arriving, early enough that it is still going as the splash starts to leave. It is the last thing that happens before Home.

How the light is made

The gold house is used as its own mask, so a soft band of warm white slides across and is clipped to the mark's silhouette. The light only ever touches the gold, never the paper around it.

No new asset, nothing to redraw, and nothing to keep in sync if the logo ever changes. It is one element, one gradient, and one keyframe.

What is still open

  • Soft or bright, or none at all.
  • Whether the sweep runs on every launch or only the first of the day. Once a day keeps it feeling like a greeting instead of a screensaver.
  • The real timings still need a floor of about 900ms and a ceiling of about four seconds, the same as before. The sweep would have to fit inside the floor, or be skipped on a launch that is already over.
