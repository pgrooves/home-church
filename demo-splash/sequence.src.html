The welcome after the house

Second pass, and this one fixes the real problem. The welcome does rise 8pt today — but the rise and the fade are one animation on one hard ease-out curve, so 76% of the move is over by 270ms, while the words are still too faint to see it. What is left is under 2pt, crawling out over another six tenths of a second. That is why it reads as a fade in place, and why moving it later on its own changed nothing. Here the fade is short and the travel is long: the words go solid first, then keep climbing where they can be seen.

What it costs in the app

One rule in css/components.css: the welcome gets two animations instead of one, and a lift keyframe beside the rise one the house still uses. Nothing else in the file moves.

The house, the loading line, the light across the gold, the 2750ms hold and the 420ms lift off in js/splash.js, the gate on a signed out phone, and everything waiting on the splash being gone are all untouched. Under Reduce Motion the delay goes back to 140ms, since there is no house rising to follow and the still hold is only 1200ms.
