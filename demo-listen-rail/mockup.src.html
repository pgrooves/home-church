A sticky month rail for the archive

Once Archive clears the header, a second sticky strip slides in underneath it, carrying the month each episode was preached. Scroll it sideways, tap a month, and the page goes there. The phone below is live — scroll it.

How it behaves

  • Appears

    The rail is hidden through Most recent and Current series. It slides down the moment the Archive header passes under the top bar, and slides back up when you scroll above it again.

  • Tracks

    As you scroll, the active month is whichever one is currently sitting under the header. The rail auto-scrolls to keep that month centered, so the label is never off-screen.

  • Jumps

    Tap a month and the page smooth-scrolls so that month's first episode lands just below the rail. Tapping never leaves the row hidden behind the chrome.

  • Measures

    Opening an episode changes every offset below it. Positions are recomputed on expand, on resize, and on text-size changes, so the rail cannot drift out of sync.

  • Reach

    42px tall, so the rail plus the header still leave the episode list the majority of the screen. Chips clear the 44px tap minimum by their padding and the rail's full height.

One thing to decide

The archive is not in date order

It is grouped by series first, newest series first, and dates only run downward inside a group. So the page really goes Aug 2026 → May 2026 (David), then back up to Jun 2026 (Messages), then down again, then back to Oct 2025 (Ephesians). A rail of bare months would appear to jump backwards three times.

Two ways to handle it

Months only

Exactly what you asked for: month + year, in page order, nothing else. Honest about where you are, but the repeated months read as a bug unless you already know the archive is grouped by series.

Months + series markers

Same chips, with a hairline and the series name at each group seam. The second “Jun 2026” now reads as June 2026 in Messages, and the rail doubles as a series index. Toggle both above the phone.

What it touches

  • Markup

    A rail element in the shell alongside the top bar, plus a date attribute on each archive row. js/app.js, js/screens/listen.js

  • Style

    Rail, chips, seam marker, and the top bar giving up its bottom hairline while the rail is showing. css/components.css

  • Behavior

    One scroll listener, shared with the existing rAF-throttled one that already drives the logo. No new scroll handler. js/app.js

  • Scope

    Listen only. The rail hides itself on every other tab and on pushed views.
