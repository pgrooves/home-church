What actually changes

One line in css/components.css. The welcome's animation is the only thing touched: its delay, its duration, and the 8pt of travel that becomes 10.

The house, the loading line, the light across the gold, the 420ms lift off and the 2750ms hold in js/splash.js are all the numbers that are there now. The gate that comes up on a signed out phone is untouched, and so is everything that waits on the splash being gone.

The one thing to watch for

Reduce Motion. css/base.css collapses every duration to nothing but leaves delays alone, so the still version is the three pieces appearing in order, and the still hold is 1200ms. A welcome that waited until 980 would be readable for two tenths of a second.

So under Reduce Motion the delay goes back to the house's own 140ms: no house is rising, so there is nothing to follow, and all three pieces arrive at once the way that version was always meant to.
