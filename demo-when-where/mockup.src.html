The header

Sunday Gatherings, under a When & Where eyebrow

The website's own heading, kept. The tile behind ••• still says When & Where, because that is what somebody is looking for in a menu, and the eyebrow over the title is where the two names meet — the same move every section header in the app makes.

The paragraph

Both sentences are the church's, lifted from the site word for word, and both are editable slots: turn on Edit mode and tap them. Everyone is welcome. Everyone is family. is bold on the site and stays bold here, because it is the line doing the work.

Service times

Every Sunday, then the three times

service_day and service_times, the columns Home's gathering card already reads. The site writes them on one line as “8AM 9:30AM & 11AM”; here they are three separated items, so a fourth service is a row in Supabase and nothing else, and the numbers line up in tabular figures.

Location

The address, and the one thing the app can do that the site can't

address_line1 over town / state / zip, then maps_url behind Get directions — Apple Maps on a phone, the web map everywhere else. Secondary rather than primary: it is the only button on the screen and a filled black bar would shout over the address it sits under.

The photographs

The newest three off Instagram

The site closes with three photographs — a baptism, the room with hands up, the kids' class. The arrangement is the site's, and what shipped fills it with the three most recent Instagram posts, already mirrored into Storage by the sync job that feeds the rail on Connect. So there is no second place to keep three pictures current. Three or none: two in a grid built for three is a hole, and a page that ends on the address is a perfectly good page.

What was decided

  1. The photographs are in, and they are the Instagram ones rather than three uploaded files: a photograph frozen into a page is two years old by the time anybody notices.
  2. Not white on black. The site sets this section in white on near-black. The app has one paper and one dark theme and they follow the phone, so this page wears whichever the reader is already in. Inverting one screen inside the app would read as a mistake rather than as the website.
  3. Nothing is typed in. Times and address come from church_profile, the row Home already reads, so the Sunday a service moves this page moves with it. The two paragraphs are Edit-mode slots.
  4. Home keeps its gathering card. Same times, same Directions button, still on the first screen. This is the fuller answer behind •••, not a replacement for it.
