Home Church, before the Home tab

Four ways to open the door

Same screen in all four: the gold house, a welcome, and the word that says the app is still catching up. What changes is the ground it sits on, the voice the welcome is set in, and how much light is in the room. Nothing here is wired into the app yet. Every phone runs the real tokens from css/tokens.css at real iPhone points, so what you pick is what ships.

Study D

Threshold

Light spills in from the top of the screen, the house is the biggest it gets, and a short gold rule draws itself under it before the welcome arrives. Loading is set as a tracked label, the same style the app uses for eyebrows, so it sits down at the bottom as furniture.

What is the same in all four

  • The mark alone, no lockup. The words are two lines below it anyway, so the wordmark would say Home Church twice.
  • Welcome home, Trey. signed in, Welcome home. signed out. First name only, from the profile, and never a fallback like "friend".
  • Loading... at the bottom, small, in the muted token, never in gold. It is the least important thing on the screen.
  • Everything fades up in sequence: house, then welcome, then the loading line breathing on a slow cycle. All of it collapses to a still screen when Reduce Motion is on.

How long you would see it

The splash stays up until the app has read the signed in profile and painted Home, then cross fades out over about 240ms. Cold on a phone that has the app cached, that is roughly half a second.

It needs a floor, or a fast launch turns it into a flicker. Holding it for a minimum of about 900ms is what makes it read as a greeting rather than a stutter. It also needs a ceiling, so a phone with no signal gives up after about four seconds and goes to Home anyway.

The one thing to decide first

The name. The splash can only greet you by name if the profile is already on the phone, which it is on every launch after the first. On a genuinely cold first launch it would say Welcome home. and stop there rather than pop a name in halfway through.

Pick a study and the rest is small: it becomes a fixed layer in index.html painted before any script runs, plus the matching background in capacitor.config.json so the native launch screen and this one are the same color.
